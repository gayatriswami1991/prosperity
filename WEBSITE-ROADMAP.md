## 5. Platform recommendation (decision needed)

- **WordPress + Elementor/Bricks**: easiest for the team to edit, best for blog/SEO, big plugin ecosystem. Recommended.
- Webflow: prettier, costlier, harder for non-designers to maintain.
- Custom code: only if you want full control and have a developer.

## 6. Conversion plan

Primary action everywhere: book a Clarity Call. Secondary: a free scorecard or calculator. One CTA style, sticky mobile bar, no pop-ups, no fake scarcity. Track: page view, video play, scorecard start/finish, booking click, form submit, call attended.

## 7. Compliance guardrails

No guaranteed returns or income. Show only verified credentials (15+ years, 12,000+, 500+ need proof). Do not call the business a SEBI-registered adviser unless that registration exists and is displayed. Keep footer disclaimers. Meta employment/financial-products ad categories apply to ads.
