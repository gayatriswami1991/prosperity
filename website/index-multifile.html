Dr Maria Pramila Dsouza

Chartered Wealth Manager & Prosperity Mentor

From a government school teacher to a financial educator and wealth professional, Dr Maria’s journey has been driven by one mission: to make practical financial education understandable, actionable and accessible to families.

Her approach combines financial education with practical implementation across money management, protection, investing, retirement, income creation and legacy planning. She is the founder of Prosperity Life School, an ecosystem designed to help people move from financial confusion towards greater clarity, confidence and financial capability.
