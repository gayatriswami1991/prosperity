Her story

Financial Education That Families Can Actually Use

Dr Maria began as a government school teacher. She went on to earn a PhD in Finance, became a Chartered Wealth Manager, and built Prosperity Life School to give every family the money education schools never taught.

Her approach combines financial education with practical implementation across money management, protection, investing, retirement, income creation and legacy planning. She helps people move from financial confusion towards greater clarity, confidence and financial capability.

  • PhD in Finance and Chartered Wealth Manager
  • Creator of the GEM Framework: Grow Awareness, Elevate Wealth, Master Life Skills
  • Guides families in India and the Gulf
  • Teaches in English, Kannada, Tulu and Konkani
