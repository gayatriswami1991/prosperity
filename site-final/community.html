Questions answered

Before You Join

Is the community free to join?

You can start free by joining the WhatsApp group and attending the free live masterclass. Paid memberships are optional and open every month.

I am not good with numbers. Is this for me?

Yes. Everything is taught with simple examples from everyday family life, with no jargon.

Will you tell me which stocks to buy?

No. This is education, not tips. You learn how to make your own sound decisions.

I don’t earn a lot. Can I still join?

Yes. The system works at any income. Most families find money leaks in the first session.

Which languages are the sessions in?

Sessions are taught in English and Kannada, and Dr Maria also teaches in Tulu and Konkani.

I live abroad. Can I join?

Yes. The community and masterclasses are online, and many members are NRI families in the Gulf and India.
